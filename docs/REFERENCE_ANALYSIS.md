# Super Cars II — reference notes

TussieCars is an original game. These notes separate what was actually checked from the design memory that shaped it. No graphics, maps, or music from Super Cars II are in this project.

## Verified

Checked against the Wikipedia article for Super Cars II. MobyGames and Lemon Amiga returned 403, and the Wikipedia gameplay section was essentially empty, so the list below is short on purpose.

- Released in 1991.
- Developed by Magnetic Fields and published by Gremlin Graphics.
- Music by Barry Leitch.
- Platforms include Amiga and Atari ST. An MS-DOS release followed in 1996 as Super Cars International.
- It is the sequel to Super Cars (1990).
- It is a top-down racing game with a single-player mode and a multiplayer mode.
- Contemporary scores cited on the page include ACE 812/1000, Amiga Action 93%, and CVG 91%.

Secondary commentary (not a manual) describes split-screen play and a career of earning money between races. That commentary was not treated as a move-by-move spec.

## Not verified

These are assumptions, not sourced facts. They were not copied into TussieCars as numbers or layouts.

- Exact weapon names, ammo counts, and damage tables.
- Division names, entry fees, and prize ladders.
- How many laps, checkpoints, or rivals the original used.
- The original car handling curve, camera height, and upgrade prices.
- The original track geometry.

## What this game took from the idea

- A top-down view, a small field, and short races.
- Cars that slide, hit each other, and can be wrecked.
- Weapons during the race, then a garage between rounds.
- Prize money that matters.
- A season you can continue later.
- Two players on one machine when the screen can split.

Track shapes, car bodies, palette, music, and the economy in `data/balance.json` are new.
